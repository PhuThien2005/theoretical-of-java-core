# 20 - Comparable Va Comparator

## Can nam

- `Comparable` dinh nghia thu tu tu nhien cua object.
- `Comparator` dinh nghia cach sap xep tu ben ngoai.
- Hay dung voi `Collections.sort`, `List.sort`, `TreeSet`, `TreeMap`.

## Tu kiem tra

- `compareTo()` tra ve so am, 0, so duong co y nghia gi?
- Khi nao dung `Comparable`, khi nao dung `Comparator`?
- Sap xep nhieu tieu chi lam the nao?
