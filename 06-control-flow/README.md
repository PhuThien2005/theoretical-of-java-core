# 06 - Cau Truc Dieu Khien

## Re nhanh

- `if`
- `if else`
- `else if`
- `switch`
- switch expression

## Vong lap

- `for`
- enhanced for
- `while`
- `do while`

## Lenh dieu khien

- `break`
- `continue`
- `return`

## Tu kiem tra

- Khi nao dung `for`, khi nao dung `while`?
- `break` va `continue` khac nhau nhu the nao?
- Switch expression co diem gi khac switch statement?
